BendLab: Trained Inverse Model (research prototype, not peer reviewed)

One neural network ensemble, trained on 100,000 simulated BendLab seedlings with curves truncated at random hours, predicts the hidden parameters from a bending curve observed for any length of time. Paste a curve, then drag the observation window to see what can be known by each hour.

What can be known by each hour?

Median prediction errors on 12,000 held-out simulated plants, by observation window. One flexible any-hour network is used, so a dedicated single-window model can be somewhat sharper; the shape of knowledge over time is the point.

tau reveals itself early, phi0 sharpens steadily, k settles late, and max_rate stays unknown at every window: when the bend-rate cap never binds, no amount of extra observation creates the missing information. With 1 degree of independent noise on every reading, full-day errors rise to roughly 27%. Training ranges: k 0.05 to 3, tau 0.25 to 6 h, max_rate 2 to 40 deg/h, phi0 5 to 85 deg.

Method: trained any-hour neural network ensemble (weights embedded in this page) with a BendLab equation self-check. It is not a curve fit and not fitted to real plants. BendLab (open source): github.com/Yash-Tripath1/Bendlab. Educational, not peer reviewed.
