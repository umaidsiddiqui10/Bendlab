Limitations

Deterministic BendLab curve fit. Assumes an initially upright, stationary seedling under constant light. A good fit does not prove these are the plant's unique biological parameters, particularly when a parameter is flagged as unidentifiable.

k: light sensitivity (deg/h); tau: response lag (h); max_rate: bend-rate limit (deg/h); phi0: effective light angle (deg).

Method: BendLab equations with bounded least-squares fitting, not an AI prediction.  ·  Model: BendLab (open source), github.com/Yash-Tripath1/Bendlab · Educational, not peer reviewed.
